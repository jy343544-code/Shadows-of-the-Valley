# SHADOWS OF THE VALLEY — GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT

## High concept
A third-person Egyptian dark-fantasy action adventure starring Sekhem, a fallen royal guardian. Combat focuses on fast melee strings, aerial launchers, evasive movement, and a supernatural shadow tether. These are broad action-game mechanics; characters, lore, weapons, and visual identity remain original.

## World and story
The fictional kingdom of Akhet draws inspiration from ancient Egyptian architecture and desert landscapes. A mysterious Sunstone once powered its sacred temples. After it vanished, daylight faded and creatures emerged from sealed tombs.

**Sekhem** is a former royal guard, burdened by the loss of his family during the Night of Silence.

## Combat identity: Flow of the Shadow Blade
- Three-hit combo: quick cut, advancing reverse cut, rising finisher.
- The finisher launches lighter enemies for potential aerial follow-ups in a later milestone.
- Dodge and readable enemy telegraphs reward timing.
- Block, parry, lock-on, and aerial follow-ups are planned after the first playable combat loop.
- **Shadow Tether:** a spectral binding that pulls smaller enemies toward Sekhem or interrupts them. Heavy monsters resist it.

## Mobile controls
- Left virtual joystick: movement.
- Right drag region: camera.
- Main attack: combo chain.
- Separate buttons planned for dodge and Shadow Tether.
- Optional target lock planned for crowded encounters.

## Monsters
1. **Tombbound:** wrapped tomb guardian; slow, clear melee attacks; tutorial enemy.
2. **Ash Stalker:** quick ash creature that circles and punishes stationary players.
3. **Basalt Brute:** massive stone-and-bronze guardian; resists tether and launch.
4. **Sand Wraith:** ranged spirit firing compressed sand shards, then relocating.
5. **The Gatekeeper:** first boss; broad swings, ground shockwaves, then an exposed weak point.

## First chapter: The Black Gate
Target first playable slice: 10–15 minutes.
1. The Drowned Dunes — movement and camera tutorial.
2. Outer Gate — fight two Tombbound.
3. Inscribed Passage — simple environmental interaction.
4. Broken Courtyard — introduce Shadow Tether and a mixed encounter.
5. Gatekeeper Chamber — boss encounter and story reveal.

Opening line: “The curse was never inside the stone. It was inside those who guarded it.”

## Visual direction
Sun-bleached sandstone, black basalt, oxidized bronze, muted lapis accents, strong silhouettes, readable attack telegraphs, and restrained particles. Original monster silhouettes; no copied characters or recognizable copyrighted designs.

## Performance target
Mid-range Android phones with 4–6 GB RAM. Use URP, baked lighting, limited real-time shadows, compressed textures, modest polygon counts, and compact encounter spaces.

## First prototype scope
One player, one three-hit combo, one Tombbound monster with basic chase/melee AI, one Shadow Tether ability, a small greybox arena, and mobile touch controls. No open world, multiplayer, or complex inventory until this loop is stable.
