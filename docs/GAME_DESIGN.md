# SHADOWS OF THE VALLEY — GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT

## High concept
A third-person Egyptian dark-fantasy action adventure. The player controls Sekhem, a fallen royal guardian, as he enters a buried temple to uncover why the sun has disappeared.

The game takes inspiration from the broad genre of cinematic action adventures, while using original characters, lore, enemies, weapons, and visual identity.

## World
The fictional kingdom of Akhet is inspired by ancient Egyptian architecture, desert landscapes, monumental stonework, and funerary symbolism. It is not a recreation of a specific historical dynasty.

A mysterious artifact called the Sunstone once powered the kingdom's sacred temples. After it vanished, daylight faded and creatures emerged from sealed tombs.

## Main character
**Sekhem** — a former royal guard. Quiet, disciplined, and burdened by the loss of his family during the Night of Silence.

## First chapter: The Black Gate
Target duration: 10–15 minutes for the first playable slice.

1. **The Drowned Dunes** — movement and camera tutorial.
2. **Outer Gate** — first encounter with basic tomb guardians.
3. **Inscribed Passage** — a simple environmental interaction.
4. **Broken Courtyard** — combat encounter and introduction to the special ability.
5. **Gatekeeper Chamber** — first boss encounter and story reveal.

Opening line:
> The curse was never inside the stone. It was inside those who guarded it.

## Core combat
- Light attack: fast, low commitment.
- Heavy attack: slower, higher damage and stagger.
- Dodge: short evasive movement.
- Block / parry: planned for a later milestone.
- Special ability: consumes Shadow Power.

Prototype scope begins with one weapon, one light attack, one enemy type, and one small arena.

## First enemies
- Tomb Guard: slow, readable melee attacks.
- Shade Warrior: faster enemy, planned after the first prototype.
- Stone Colossus: heavy elite enemy, planned for later chapters.

## First boss
**The Gatekeeper** — an ancient stone sentinel.
- Phase 1: broad melee swings.
- Phase 2: ground shockwave.
- Phase 3: exposes a glowing weak point after a committed attack.

## Mobile UX
- Left virtual joystick for movement.
- Right-side drag area for camera.
- Large attack button.
- Contextual dodge and ability buttons added after the basic combat loop is stable.
- Adjustable sensitivity and button size are planned.

## Visual direction
- Sun-bleached sandstone, black basalt, oxidized bronze, muted lapis accents.
- Strong silhouettes and readable enemy telegraphs.
- Controlled particle effects and limited real-time lights for mobile performance.

## Performance target
Mid-range Android devices with 4–6 GB RAM. Start with URP, baked lighting, limited shadow-casting lights, compressed textures, modest polygon counts, and small encounter spaces.

## Explicit scope limits for the first playable build
No open world, multiplayer, online account system, complex inventory, or multiple weapons until the first combat encounter works reliably.
